\section{Analytical and Algorithmic Details}

\subsection{Free-Jones EFIM and Closed-Form Derivatives}

Let $\bchi=[\bp^{\mathsf T},\Delta t]^{\mathsf T}$ denote position and
clock.  At the true parameters, the geometric derivative block is
$\bD_\chi=[\partial(\bA\bx)/\partial\chi_i]_{i=1}^{4}$.  The full real
parameter vector is
$\bvt=[\bchi^{\mathsf T},\Real\{\bx\}^{\mathsf T},
\operatorname{Im}\{\bx\}^{\mathsf T}]^{\mathsf T}$.  For
$\by\sim\mathcal{CN}(\bA(\bchi)\bx,\sigma^2\bI)$, the mean Jacobian with
respect to these real coordinates is
\begin{equation}
 \bD_{\vartheta}=\big[\,\bD_\chi,\ \bA,\ j\bA\,\big].
 \label{eq:s_efim_jac}
\end{equation}
The last two blocks span over $\R$ the complex column space
$\coll(\bA)$ viewed as a real subspace.  Because this subspace is closed
under multiplication by $j$, its projector under the real inner product
$\Real\{\ba^{\mathsf H}\bv\}$ equals the usual complex projector
$\bA\bA^\dagger$.  Eliminating the real and imaginary Jones coordinates
projects the geometric derivatives onto the complement of $\coll(\bA)$:
\begin{equation}
 \bJ_\chi^{\rm free}
 =\frac{2}{\sigma^2}\Real\!\left\{\bD_\chi^{\mathsf H}
 \big(\bI-\bA\bA^\dagger\big)\bD_\chi\right\}.
 \label{eq:s_efim}
\end{equation}
This is the generalized Schur complement of the Jones nuisance block.  The
Moore--Penrose inverse makes it valid when $\bA$ is rank deficient; it reduces
to the ordinary Schur complement when the nuisance information block is
invertible.

The PEB and CEB use analytic Jacobian columns.  Put $\nu_n=n-(N-1)/2$ and
$\bN_\nu=\bI_T\otimes\diag(\nu_0,\ldots,\nu_{N-1})\otimes\bI_I$.  The common
clock orbit of main-paper Eq.~\eqref{main-eq:orbit_short} gives
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\partial(\bA\bx)}{\partial\Delta t}
 =-j2\pi\Delta f\,\bN_\nu\bA\bx .
 \label{eq:s_clock_jac}
\end{equation}
For position coordinate $p_i$, define
$\boldsymbol\delta_k=[\delta_{k,1},\ldots,\delta_{k,M_R}]^{\mathsf T}$ and
use the aperture derivative in main-paper Eq.~\eqref{main-eq:apertjac}.  The
remaining factor derivatives and the product rule are
\begin{align}
 \frac{\partial\bd_k}{\partial p_i}
 &=-j2\pi\Delta f\,\diag(\nu_0,\ldots,\nu_{N-1})\bd_k
 \frac{[\bR_k^{\mathsf T}\bs_{{\rm UR},k}]_i}{c_0},\nonumber\\
 \frac{\partial\bh_k}{\partial p_i}
 &=\bOmega_k\!\left[\bg_k\odot
 \left(-j\kappa\frac{\partial\boldsymbol\delta_k}{\partial p_i}\right)
 \right],\nonumber\\
 \frac{\partial\bA_k}{\partial p_i}
 &=\left(\frac{\partial\bh_k}{\partial p_i}\otimes\bd_k
 +\bh_k\otimes\frac{\partial\bd_k}{\partial p_i}\right)\otimes\bBm_k .
 \label{eq:s_position_jac}
\end{align}
The $i$th position column of $\bD_\chi$ is
$\sum_k(\partial\bA_k/\partial p_i)\bx_k$.  In the parameter-independent
compressed coordinates, replace
$(\bA,\bD_\chi,\bBm_k)$ by
$(\bU_S^{\mathsf H}\bA,\bU_S^{\mathsf H}\bD_\chi,
\bQ_S^{\mathsf H}\bBm_k)$, respectively.  The unknown variance does not add
an EFIM correction: for circular Gaussian noise, its Fisher cross block with
all mean parameters is zero.

The absorbed carrier phase $e^{-j2\pi f_c\tau_k}$ includes the common
range phase $e^{-j\kappa d_{{\rm UR},k}}$.  It and any smooth pathwise
scalar amplitude factor multiply $\bA_k\bx_k$ by a scalar.  Their
position derivatives lie in $\coll(\bA)$ and vanish after projection by
$\bPi_{\bA}^{\perp}$; omitting them from~\eqref{eq:s_position_jac} is
exact for free complex $\bx_k$.

Substitution of~\eqref{eq:s_clock_jac} into~\eqref{eq:s_efim} gives the
free-Jones clock information,
\begin{align}
 J_{tt}^{\rm free}
 &=\frac{2}{\sigma^2}(2\pi\Delta f)^2
 \big\|\bPi_{\bA}^{\perp}\bN_\nu\bA\bx\big\|_2^2,\nonumber\\
 {\rm CEB}
 &\ge \frac{\sigma}{2\sqrt{2}\,\pi\Delta f\,
 \|\bPi_{\bA}^{\perp}\bN_\nu\bA\bx\|_2}.
 \label{eq:s_clock_info}
\end{align}
The second line accounts for the position nuisance through the Schur
complement; equality holds when the clock column is EFIM-orthogonal to the
position columns.  The same tone-index operator $\bN_\nu$ appears in the
conditional clock profile of C3.

\subsection{Phase-II Normal Matrix and Envelope Gradient}

Let $s_k=\tfrac12\tr\bG_{0,kk}$ and partition
$\bA_0=[\bA_{0,1},\ldots,\bA_{0,K}]$ into its two-column Jones blocks.

\begin{lemma}[Anchor-column test for positive definiteness]
\label{lem:s_glambda}
Suppose every anchor $\hat\be_k$ is nonzero and $\lambda_ks_k>0$.  Define
\begin{equation}
 \bZ_{\hat e}=\big[\bA_{0,1}\hat\be_1,\ldots,
 \bA_{0,K}\hat\be_K\big].
 \label{eq:s_anchor_columns}
\end{equation}
Then $\bG_\lambda\succ\mathbf0$ if and only if
$\rank\bZ_{\hat e}=K$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{IEEEproof}
For $\bx=[\bx_1^{\mathsf T},\ldots,\bx_K^{\mathsf T}]^{\mathsf T}$,
\begin{equation}
 \bx^{\mathsf H}\bG_\lambda\bx
 =\|\bA_0\bx\|_2^2+
 \sum_{k=1}^{K}\lambda_ks_k
 \|\bPi_{\hat\be_k}^{\perp}\bx_k\|_2^2.
 \label{eq:s_pd_quadratic}
\end{equation}
The right-hand side is zero exactly when
$\bx_k=a_k\hat\be_k$ for some $a_k\in\C$ and
$\bZ_{\hat e}[a_1,\ldots,a_K]^{\mathsf T}=\mathbf0$.  The quadratic
form has no nonzero null vector exactly when $\bZ_{\hat e}$ has column
rank $K$.
\end{IEEEproof}

Lemma~\ref{lem:s_glambda} turns the nullspace condition of main-paper
Eq.~\eqref{main-eq:glambda_pd} into a pointwise rank test.  In the scalar
receiver mode, $\bS_1\bTheta_k^{(6)}\hat\be_k\ne\mathbf0$ is necessary for
each anchor column to survive, and the $K$ surviving columns must additionally
remain jointly independent.  The resulting rank test costs $O(M_rK^2)$ at
any candidate position.

For the analytic Phase-II position gradient, fix a clock branch and put
$\hat\bx=\bG_\lambda^{-1}\bb$ and write $\partial_i=\partial/\partial p_i$.
Differentiating $Q=\bb^{\mathsf H}\bG_\lambda^{-1}\bb$ without
differentiating $\hat\bx$ explicitly gives
\begin{equation}
 \partial_iQ=2\Real\{\hat\bx^{\mathsf H}\partial_i\bb\}
 -\Real\{\hat\bx^{\mathsf H}(\partial_i\bG_\lambda)\hat\bx\}.
 \label{eq:s_envelope_grad}
\end{equation}
With the refreshed anchors held fixed during Phase~II, every term is
available from the response derivatives above:
\begin{align}
 \partial_i\bb
 &=(\partial_i\bA_0)^{\mathsf H}\bU_{\rm clk}^{\mathsf H}\by,\nonumber\\
 \partial_i\bG_0
 &=(\partial_i\bA_0)^{\mathsf H}\bA_0
 +\bA_0^{\mathsf H}(\partial_i\bA_0),\nonumber\\
 \partial_i\bR_{{\rm dir},kk}
 &=\lambda_k\Real\!\left\{\tr\!\left(
 \bA_{0,k}^{\mathsf H}\partial_i\bA_{0,k}\right)\right\}
 \bPi_{\hat\be_k}^{\perp}.
 \label{eq:s_envelope_terms}
\end{align}
At a unique interior clock maximizer, main-paper Eq.~\eqref{main-eq:envelope}
gives $\nabla\widetilde F=-\nabla Q$.  The implementation divides $F$ and
its gradient by $M=INT$ without changing the optimizer.  It reuses
$\hat\bx$ and the normal-matrix factorization.  The Gram derivatives follow
the Kronecker products in main-paper Eq.~\eqref{main-eq:gram_factorization};
no additional $2K$-dimensional factorization is needed.

\subsection{Coupled Factor Recovery}

The coupled recovery uses the balanced Hankel window
$P=\lceil N/2\rceil$ and $L=N+1-P$.  Its anti-diagonal multiplicity
$w_n$ counts the pairs $(p,\ell)$ satisfying $p+\ell=n$.  After absorbing the
centered-tone column scalar into the recovered factor, write
$[\bD]_{n,k}=z_k^n$.  Let $\bY_{{\rm d},t}\in\C^{r\times N}$ denote the
compressed receive--tone slice at configuration $t$.  Define its balanced
Hankel lift by
\[
 [\mathcal Z]_{i,p,\ell,t}=[\bY_{{\rm d},t}]_{i,p+\ell},
 \qquad 0\le p<P,\quad 0\le\ell<L,
\]
and denote its row-$(i,t)$, column-$(p,\ell)$ matricization by
$\mathcal Z_{(i,t),(p,\ell)}\in\C^{rT\times PL}$.  For each estimated pole
$z_k$, the implementation constructs
$[\bv_{P,k}]_p=z_k^p$ and $[\bv_{L,k}]_\ell=z_k^\ell$ from that same pole;
the $k$th lifted design column is $\bv_{P,k}\otimes\bv_{L,k}$.  Hence
\begin{align}
 [\bD_{\rm lift}]_{(p,\ell),k}&=z_k^{p+\ell},\nonumber\\
 \bD_{\rm lift}^{\mathsf T}\bD_{\rm lift}^{\ast}
 &=\bD^{\mathsf T}\bW\bD^{\ast},\nonumber\\
 \mathcal Z_{(i,t),(p,\ell)}\bD_{\rm lift}^{\ast}
 &=\bY_{rT,N}\bW\bD^{\ast}.
 \label{eq:s_lift_equivalence}
\end{align}
The two equalities in~\eqref{eq:s_lift_equivalence} give the weighted
matrix-domain normal equations of main-paper
Eq.~\eqref{main-eq:coupledls_short}, with identical diagonal loading.  Both
Vandermonde factors are powers of one $z_k$: each design column depends on
one delay.  The lift changes residual weighting but introduces neither a
second delay nor a separate factor projection.

\subsection{Information and Compression Scope}

For a real geometric direction $\bu$, free-Jones information has the
variational representation
\begin{equation}
 \bu^{\mathsf T}\bJ_\chi^{\rm free}\bu
 =\frac{2}{\sigma^2}\min_{\delta\bx}
 \left\|\bD_\chi\bu+\bA\delta\bx\right\|_2^2.
 \label{eq:s_nested_info}
\end{equation}
Adding a nested set of receive components appends nonnegative residual terms
to this minimized quadratic form and hence cannot decrease the free-Jones
information.  This aggregate order remains valid even when a receiver mode
makes an individual Jones block singular; a single-panel scalar information
ratio alone need not order a multi-panel EFIM.

The likelihood isometry in main-paper Theorem~\ref{main-thm:compress} is a
pointwise deterministic identity for every observation and candidate.
Interpreting the compressed observation as a sufficient statistic and
preserving the stated EFIM additionally use the matched white-Gaussian model.
With colored covariance, whitening or the corresponding covariance-weighted
projection is required; that case is outside the theorem.

More generally, any parameter-independent semi-unitary map whose range
contains both $\coll(\bA)$ and $\coll(\bD_\chi)$ preserves the free-Jones
information by the same argument.  The calibrated Maxwell--Kronecker basis
is fixed without UE-dependent quantities.  Preservation of
the noiseless delay poles further requires distinct nonzero paths, adequate
frequency aperture, and rank $K$ of the compressed receive--configuration
snapshot matrix.  These acquisition conditions are separate from likelihood
sufficiency.

\section{Phase-I Complexity by Block}

Let $N_{\rm pk}$ be the number of retained angular peaks, $N_r$ the exact
range-scan length, $N_\pi$ the number of shortlisted permutations, $N_s$ the
number of exact local starts, and $I_{\rm ex}$ the objective/gradient
evaluations used per start. Let $F_x,F_y$ be the zero-padded spatial FFT bin
counts along the panel axes, $F_{\rm b}=F_xF_y$ their product, and $G_r$ the
number of beam-space trial ranges.
Receive compression costs $O(IrNT)$.  The implemented delay solver forms and
eigendecomposes the $N\times N$ snapshot covariance, costing
$O(N^2rT+N^3)$; its forward--backward total-least-squares (FB-TLS) step
adds $O(NK^2+K^3)$.  A direct thin singular value decomposition (SVD)
would instead cost $O(NrT\min\{N,rT\})$ for the subspace.

Constructing and solving the coupled normal equations for the balanced
$P\times L$ lift costs
$O(rTPLK+PLK^2+rTK^2+K^3)$, followed by
$O(KrT\min\{r,T\})$ for the $K$ rank-one splits.  For each panel--column
pair, beam-space proposals and exact candidate re-scoring cost
\begin{equation}
 C_{\rm pair}=O\!\left(TM_R+G_rF_{\rm b}\log F_{\rm b}
 +N_{\rm pk}(N_rM_R+TM_R)\right).
 \label{eq:s_pair_cost}
\end{equation}
After the element-domain adjoint, the $N_r$ range tests
cost $O(N_rM_R)$ per peak, without repeating the $T$ configurations.
Candidate generation covers all $K^2$ panel--column pairs.  Multi-start
exact spherical refinement is restricted to at most $N_\pi K$ distinct
pairs from the shortlisted permutations and costs
$O(N_\pi K N_s I_{\rm ex}TM_R)$; permutation arithmetic costs $O(KK!)$.
When enabled, a fixed-count dictionary of size $|\mathcal C|$ adds
$O(K|\mathcal C|TM_R+K^2|\mathcal C|T)$ for cached panel responses and
pairwise correlations.  The $S$ common-geometry starts cost
$O(SI_gKTM_R)$ over $I_g$ evaluations per start.  The shortlist contains
$N_\pi=2$ permutations;
Table~\ref{tab:s_settings} lists the other fixed budgets.
Refreshing the Jones anchors at $\hat\bp_{\rm GI}$ requires one conditional
clock profile---approximately one $C_{\rm outer}$ in main-paper
Eq.~\eqref{main-eq:cost}---and introduces no outer iteration.

\section{Focused Numerical Audits}

The reported p95 is the nearest-rank empirical 95th percentile.

\subsection{Compression}

The calibrated aggregate subspace has numerical rank $r=2K=6$ in every
operating-region trial.  $\sigma_j$ denotes the $j$th singular value of the
forward--backward augmented tone--snapshot matrix used for delay-subspace
estimation. At $-10$~dB, the $I=96$ to $r=6$ projection
changes the delay-subspace leakage $\sigma_{K+1}/\sigma_K$ from $0.824$ to
$0.357$.  The p95 Phase-I delay error changes from $3.96$ to $0.156$~ns.

For an intentionally unwhitened mismatch test at $-10$~dB, receive-noise
correlation across all $I$ receive channels in their stacked order follows
$[\bR]_{ij}=\rho^{|i-j|}$; tones and configurations remain white.  At
$\rho=0,0.5,0.8$, the proposed route has catastrophic rates
$0.83\%,0.42\%,0.21\%$, versus $1.04\%,1.88\%,2.71\%$ for the raw-delay
control.  That control estimates the delay subspace from the uncompressed
observation and leaves later stages unchanged.  The criterion is not
covariance weighted, so these results do not extend the matched-white-noise
sufficiency statement.

\subsection{Full-EVS Directional Weight}

A paired full-EVS sweep uses $\lambda_k\in\{0,0.1,1,10\}$, five SNRs and
$480$ common-initializer trials per cell.  The largest movements across
weights are $6.23\times10^{-6}$~mm in position and
$3.76\times10^{-5}$~ps in clock; every catastrophic indicator is unchanged.
The largest penalty/data-objective ratio is $6.45\times10^{-6}$.  In
Eq.~\eqref{eq:s_pd_quadratic}, the penalty term vanishes along each refreshed
anchor direction.  Lemma~\ref{lem:s_glambda} gives the rank condition for
the remaining nullspace to vanish and $\bG_\lambda\succ\mathbf0$.

\subsection{Clock Certificates}

Across the $4\,320$ operating-region trials, all $35\,696$ conditional clock
profiles returned a certified gap at or below the prescribed tolerance.
None reached the subdivision cap, and the Powell safeguard was never
selected.  Panel association was correct in every trial; two trials were
catastrophic.

\subsection{Model-Boundary Diagnostics}

\emph{Receiver calibration.}
In a separate generation-only audit at $-10$~dB, each mismatch level has
$480$ paired trials.  EVS log-gain standard deviation reaches $0.1$, EVS
phase error $10^\circ$, and BS sensor-coordinate error $0.5$~mm.  Over these
levels, conditional position RMSE is at most $0.497$~mm, compared with
$0.416$~mm without mismatch.

\emph{Spatial wideband.}
Each tone in the spatial-wideband diagnostic uses
$f_n=f_c+\nu_n\Delta f$ and $\lambda_n=c_0/f_n$. The physical arrays,
panel geometry and RIS codes remain fixed, and estimation uses the
center-frequency model. Nine prespecified noiseless samples (three
positions, three realizations) initialize local refinement at the truth;
Phase~I is not run. Position bias is $0.0037$--$0.0804$~mm, at most
$0.171$ of each sample's $-10$-dB PEB. Clock bias is
$0.19$--$5.38$~ps; four samples exceed their $-10$-dB CEB.
